About

Machine learning and wireless communications, with emphasis on distributed intelligence and efficient information processing in edge systems.

Vukan Ninkovic received his BSc and MSc degrees in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the Faculty of Technical Sciences, University of Novi Sad, Serbia, in 2018 and 2019, respectively, where he was awarded the Telenor Foundation "Professor Ilija Stojanovic" Award for the best BSc graduate in communications and signal processing. He obtained his PhD in Power, Electronic, and Communication Engineering in November 2024 from the same institution.

He is currently a Teaching Assistant/Postdoc at the Faculty of Technical Sciences and a Research Associate at the Institute for Artificial Intelligence Research and Development of Serbia. His research focuses on the design of integrated machine learning and communication systems for distributed intelligence over wireless networks. He is particularly interested in bridging learning and communication theory to enable scalable, real-time physical AI systems under practical deployment constraints.

Degrees
2019–
2024
Ph.D., Power, Electronic and Communication Engineering
Faculty of Technical Sciences, University of Novi Sad, Serbia

Defended 01/11/2024. Supervisor: Prof. Dejan Vukobratović.

"Machine learning-based improvement of the physical layer design in wireless communication systems"
Applied machine learning to the design and optimization of the physical layer in wireless communication systems, spanning block-level and end-to-end approaches. At the receiver level, deep learning methods were developed for synchronization tasks, including packet detection and carrier frequency offset estimation. At the system level, autoencoder-based architectures enabled end-to-end communication design, with emphasis on unequal error protection and robust coding for non-standard scenarios — contributing to reduced algorithmic complexity and data-driven design of next-generation wireless systems.
